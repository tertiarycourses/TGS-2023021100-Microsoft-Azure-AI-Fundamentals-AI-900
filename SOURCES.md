## Material decisions

- The 232-slide v9 deck is the coverage floor; v10.0 contains those 232 mappings plus ten lab bridge slides.
- Product terminology and UI guidance were refreshed against the pinned MicrosoftLearning commits.
- Legacy AutoML material is retained where there is no direct current replacement and is clearly identified by source lineage.
- The retired Anomaly Detector exercise is taught as a measurable anomaly-evidence and migration scenario, paired with current Content Safety issue reporting.
- Microsoft retired the AI-900 exam on 30 June 2026. The course retains its registered WSQ title and learning outcomes while avoiding claims that the exam is currently available.
- No mock-test material is included.
